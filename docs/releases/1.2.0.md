# Pustaka 1.2.0

Illustrations you can open full screen and zoom into, bookmarks next to your highlights, "Continue reading" on Series Details, a Dark grey reader theme, the reader's own brightness, and see-through reader bars.

## Installing

Download `Pustaka-1.2.0.apk` below and open it on your phone.
- **Updating from 1.0.0 or 1.1.0:** install it over the old version. Your library, progress, highlights, bookmarks and edits are kept.
- **New install:** see the [1.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/releases/tag/v1.0.0) for the first steps.

Requires Android 10 or newer.

## What's new

**Illustrations** ([#50](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/50))
- A long press on a picture in a book opens it full screen. Pinch or double-tap to zoom in, and drag to move around. Back or the close button returns to the same page.
- When the book has the picture at more than one size, the largest is opened. Line drawings on a see-through background are shown on white, as they are on the page.
- A long press on text still selects it, even right next to a picture.
- Pictures that stand on their own lines are centred on the page.

**Bookmarks in the Highlights tab** ([#51](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/51))
- The Highlights tab has two tabs you can swipe between: Highlights and Bookmarks, each with its count.
- Bookmarks are grouped by series and book, with the chapter, the start of the page and the page number. Tap one to open the reader there, or remove it with its button.
- Search looks through both. Export is still for highlights.

**Continue reading on Series Details** ([#52](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/52))
- A "Continue reading" button opens the same volume as the series card's button. It's hidden once every volume is finished.

**Reader**
- **Dark grey theme** ([#55](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/55)): light grey text on a dark grey page, with dark grey bars and sheets. Themes are now picked from a row of swatches that show each theme's colours. Auto still follows the app's light or dark theme.
- **Brightness** ([#54](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/54)): a Brightness slider in reader settings, and in Settings > Reader settings. The reader keeps that brightness, so auto-brightness doesn't change it while you read. The first time, it starts at your phone's brightness. Your phone's own brightness comes back as soon as you leave the reader.
- **Screen stays on** ([#54](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/54)) while you're reading, until no page has been turned for 5 minutes.
- **See-through bars** ([#53](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/53)): the top and bottom bars let the page show through a little.

**Tablets** ([#56](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/56))
- The tabs in the rail down the left side are centred instead of starting at the top.

## Fixes
- Screen readers now announce the "New series" button on the Series tab. Its label was hidden from them.

## Known limitations
- English only.
- EPUB only. Books protected with DRM can't be opened.
- Only internal storage can be used for the library folder, not SD cards or cloud folders.
- No sync between devices.
- Drawings made in SVG itself, rather than a picture file, can't be opened full screen.
- The page-flip animation ([#36](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/36)) is still set aside for a later version.
