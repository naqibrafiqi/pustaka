# Pustaka 1.0.0

The first release of Pustaka, an offline EPUB reader for books that come in series, such as light novels.

## Installing

1. Download `Pustaka-1.0.0.apk` below and open it on your phone. Android may ask you to allow installing apps from your browser or file manager.
2. Open Pustaka. It asks for **"All files access"**, which it needs to read the books in your library folder. Pustaka never renames or moves your files, and only deletes them when you ask it to.
3. Copy your EPUB files into the `Pustaka` folder on your phone's internal storage, then pull down to refresh.

Requires Android 10 or newer.

## What's in it

**Your library, from a folder you manage**
- EPUBs directly in `Pustaka/` are standalone books, and each folder inside it is a series, with its EPUBs as the volumes.
- Titles, authors, covers, descriptions and volume numbers are read from the books themselves. Everything can be edited in the app, and your edits are never written back to the files.
- Volumes are put in order by their volume number, or by the numbers in their file names.
- Renamed and moved books keep their progress. Books whose files have gone are kept as missing and come back when the files do.
- The library is scanned when the app opens, when you pull down to refresh, and from Settings.

**Reading**
- Page turning by swiping or tapping the edges, or continuous scrolling.
- Table of contents, a page slider, and your place saved as you read.
- Reader settings for theme (light, sepia or dark), font, text size, line spacing and margins, shared by every book.
- At the end of a volume, the book is marked finished and the next volume is offered.
- Reading speed and estimated time left, worked out from your reading sessions.

**Highlights**
- Select text to highlight it in yellow, green, blue or pink, and add a note.
- The Highlights tab lists every highlight by series and book, with search. Tapping one opens the book at that spot.
- Export one book's highlights, or all of them, as a Markdown file through the share sheet.

**Finding and organising**
- Search series and books by name, author and genre.
- Sort series by name, recently added or recently read, and show them as cards or a compact grid.
- Create series folders, remove books and series from the library (they can be restored from Settings), or delete their files.

**Settings**
- Choose another library folder on internal storage.
- Light, dark or system theme, with Pustaka's own warm colours or your wallpaper's (Android 12 and newer).
- See how much space books, covers and app data use, and clear the cover cache.
- About Pustaka and the open-source licences.

## Known limitations
- English only.
- EPUB only. Books protected with DRM can't be opened.
- Only internal storage can be used for the library folder, not SD cards or cloud folders.
- No sync between devices.
