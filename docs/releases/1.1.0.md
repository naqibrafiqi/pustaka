# Pustaka 1.1.0

Bookmarks, a way back after jumping around a book, nicer page layout, series covers from the latest volume, layouts for tablets, and a simpler app icon.

## Installing

Download `Pustaka-1.1.0.apk` below and open it on your phone.
- **Updating from 1.0.0:** install it over the old version. Your library, progress, highlights and edits are kept.
- **New install:** see the [1.0.0 release notes](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/releases/tag/v1.0.0) for the first steps.

Requires Android 10 or newer.

## What's new

**Bookmarks** ([#37](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/37))
- A bookmark button in the reader's top bar. It's filled when the page is bookmarked; tap it to add or remove the bookmark.
- The Contents sheet has a Bookmarks tab next to the chapters. It lists each bookmark with its chapter, page, percentage and the start of the page's text. Tap one to go there.

**Back to where you were** ([#35](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/35))
- After jumping somewhere from the contents, the page slider, a bookmark or a highlight, a "Back to page 42" button takes you back to the page you were reading.
- It shows and hides with the reader's bars, and goes away once you've turned 10 pages on from the new spot.

**Page layout** ([#32](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/32), [#33](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/33), [#34](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/34))
- Paragraphs are justified. A new Justify switch in reader settings, and in Settings > Reader settings, turns it off.
- More space below the text when turning pages.
- Chapter titles are centred.

**Series covers** ([#38](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/38))
- A series shows the cover of its last volume instead of its first.
- Edit series lets you pick any volume's cover instead.

**Tablets** ([#39](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/39))
- On a tablet, an unfolded foldable or a phone on its side, the tabs move to a rail down the left side.
- Grids and lists show more across, covers stop growing, and text stays in a readable column instead of stretching across the screen.

**A simpler app icon**
- A flat open book with a green ribbon, in Pustaka's warm brown.

## Fixes
- The reader's Contents and settings sheets no longer bounce when you swipe up on them while they're fully open ([#31](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/31)).
- Screens opened from a tab no longer leave extra space at the edges or at the bottom of lists.
- An author also credited with another role, such as writing the notes, was shown as "Unknown author". Books already in your library show the author once their file changes.
- A long genre on a series card no longer squeezes the number of volumes. It's shortened with "…" instead.

## Known limitations
- English only.
- EPUB only. Books protected with DRM can't be opened.
- Only internal storage can be used for the library folder, not SD cards or cloud folders.
- No sync between devices.
- The page-flip animation ([#36](https://github.com/naqibrafiqi/pustaka/issues/36)) is set aside for a later version.
